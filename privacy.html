Privacy policy

gws-cli is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Docs, Slides, Tasks) is accessed only on the owner's own computer, at the owner's request.
  • No data is sent to, stored on, or shared with any server or third party run by this app.
  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer only.
  • Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
